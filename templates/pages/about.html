AgriTracker – Smart Farming Made Simple

AgriTracker is a web-based agricultural management and marketplace platform designed to empower farmers with digital tools for farm data management and direct market access. It helps farmers track crop cycles, manage resources efficiently, and connect directly with buyers without middlemen.

  • Digital farm data tracking and analytics
  • Direct farmer-to-buyer marketplace
  • Community knowledge sharing via blogs

Core Features of AgriTracker

01

Crop Management

Track crop cycles, planting dates, harvesting schedules, and yield history digitally.

02

Resource Monitoring

Monitor water usage, fertilizers, and pesticides to improve efficiency and reduce waste.

03

Farm Analytics

Generate reports and insights to support data-driven agricultural decisions.

04

Marketplace Listings

Farmers can list produce with quantity, price, and quality for direct buyer access.

05

Buyer Search

Buyers can search and filter crops based on location, price, and availability.

06

Direct Communication

Connect farmers and buyers via WhatsApp, email, or phone for transparent deals.

07

Community Blogs

Farmers share experiences, best practices, and real profit/loss case studies.

08

Secure Access

OTP-based login and role-based access ensure platform security and reliability.
